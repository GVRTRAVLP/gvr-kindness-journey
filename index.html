Our Mission

Through the GVR Kindness Journey, we invite kind people to contribute ₹50 which will be used for feeding poor people, distributing clothes, and supporting those in need.

When you travel with GVR_TRAVLP, our team adds ₹100 to your contribution and you receive ₹150 travel credit.

Every contributor becomes part of our Kindness Team. We capture memories, take photos, and preserve the names of people who chose kindness.

How It Works

2️⃣ Your contribution helps feed the poor
